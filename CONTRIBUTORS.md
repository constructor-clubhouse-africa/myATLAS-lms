## CAPS curriculum

The CAPS reference data in this platform was transcribed from Department of Basic Education curriculum documents and verified by a contracted CAPS curriculum specialist with 40 years of classroom experience.

If the CAPS data is wrong, the product is wrong. Every row was signed off before any teacher-facing feature was built.
