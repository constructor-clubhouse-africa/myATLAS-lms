# How we work

Everyone here is a full-time student working on this part-time. Everything below is built around that. **Your studies come first.**

---

## The rhythm

| When                       | What            | How long | Where         |
| -------------------------- | --------------- | -------- | ------------- |
| Mon, Wed, Fri by 21:00     | Async standup   | 2 min    | `#standups`   |
| Tuesday 19:00              | Team sync       | 45 min   | Discord voice |
| Sprint start, Monday 19:00 | Sprint planning | 90 min   | Discord voice |
| Sprint end, Friday 19:00   | Review + retro  | 60 min   | Discord voice |
| Anytime                    | Code review     | 24h SLA  | GitHub        |

About **2.5 hours of meetings per fortnight**. Everything else is heads-down work whenever suits you.

---

## Standups

Three times a week, async. No call.

```
Since last: MY-12 — auth middleware, PR open
Next: MY-13 — role guards
Blocked: waiting on review for MY-12
Capacity this week: ~10 hrs (test Thursday)
```

**The "Blocked" line is the point.** Say it the day you get stuck.

Missing a standup is fine. Going dark for a week is not. "No capacity this week" is a complete and acceptable standup.

---

## Sprint planning

1. **Capacity round** — each person states realistic hours out loud. Tests, assignments, shifts.
2. **Sprint goal** — one sentence. What is demonstrable at the end.
3. **Walk the tickets** — read acceptance criteria aloud, clarify.
4. **Pull, don't assign** — you take tickets to fill _your own_ stated capacity.
5. **Cut to fit** — if it doesn't fit, we cut scope now, not in week two.

Declare your real capacity, not your optimistic capacity. A plan built on wishful hours is worse than no plan.

---

## Review and retro

**Review** — demo working software. Broken or half-finished is fine, show it anyway.

**Retro** — three questions, everyone answers:

- What went well?
- What slowed us down?
- What's one thing we change next sprint?

**Exactly one change per sprint**, written into this document.

---

## Work in progress

**Maximum two tickets in progress per person.** Finish before starting.

Five people each juggling four half-done tickets is how a project reaches week 10 with nothing to demo.

---

## Exams and academic load

**Sprints pause for tests and exams. This is planned, not a failure.**

- Tell us your dates as soon as you have them
- Declare low capacity in advance — that's good practice, not letting anyone down
- If this starts hurting your studies, say so and we cut your scope immediately

---

## WIL hours

- Log as you go, not at the end
- Your **workplace mentor** signs your logbook — not Muaaz
- Confirm with your WIL coordinator that this placement is approved **before** relying on the hours
- Log what you actually worked. Never inflate

---

## What we owe each other

**Everyone:**

1. Say when you're blocked, the day you're blocked
2. Say when you're overcommitted, before the deadline
3. Review others' code within 24 hours
4. Don't go dark — two lines costs nothing
5. Ask in `#help` rather than staying stuck

**Muaaz:**

1. Sprint goals set before planning, never mid-sprint
2. Architecture reviews within 24 hours
3. No new scope added mid-sprint
4. Your studies always take priority over this project
5. Your contribution documented and credited, whatever happens to the product
